06 // UX/UI & INTERACTION DESIGN — 2026

Burg Burg – Museum Game

Museum Game Concept // Roald Web App

An interactive museum game concept aimed at children aged 7–12, designed to transform passive gallery visits into an active, collaborative discovery adventure. Built directly on top of Burg Burg's existing web app, Roald, the concept leverages its artwork-scanning technology into a clue-driven treasure hunt.

How the Game Works:
01. Lobby Sync: Children & parents launch Roald and join a shared game lobby.
02. Mystery Clues: Receive engaging riddles describing artworks across the museum.
03. Gallery Search: Actively explore the exhibitions to find the matching piece.
04. Camera Scan: Scan the artwork with Roald's camera to verify and score points.
