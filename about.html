Who am I?

My name is Sem van Dongen.

I now study Communication & Multimedia Design at Avans Breda. I am interested in understanding everything there is to know about design. Understanding the language. Understanding the people. Understanding what is possible. I have learned to use a lot of tools, from building with wood to making a website. I love working with my hands, figuring out what works and being part of what I make. I think that's what I love about making something interactive: becoming part of the project. I believe people understand things better when they are part of the experience.

Making something that helps people, starts a conversation or activates someone is what I have wanted to do since I was little. It started with music. Making music to express myself and hopefully connect with others. Art has a way of helping people. It makes me want to do more.
